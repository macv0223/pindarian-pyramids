# Fivefold Scale

*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*

This broad dragon scale is larger than a human hand and bears all five chromatic colours
at once. Red glows like banked coals along one edge, blue flickers beneath the surface in
hair-thin veins of lightning, green clouds the centre like poison trapped beneath glass,
black gleams wetly like fresh acid, and white rims the scale in permanent frost.

The colours never remain entirely still.

When the scale's power is invoked, all five flare at once around a fleeting **sixth
light** - a warm, impossible hue that seems to exist somewhere between gold, violet and
memory.

Draconic script has been burned into the reverse:

> **WE STAND AS MANY.**
> **MANY STAND AS ONE.**

The Fivefold Scale was not forged. It manifested at the conclusion of the Fivefold
Trial, where the party proved that Elderon was not preserved by uniformity, but by
distinct wills choosing to stand together.

## We Stand as Many

The scale has **3 charges**.

When a creature you can see within **30 feet** of you fails a saving throw, you can use
your **reaction** and expend 1 charge to call out that creature's name.

The creature rerolls the saving throw and must use the new result.

The scale regains all expended charges **daily at dawn**.

A creature can benefit from this property only once for the same saving throw.

## Fivefold Resilience

Whenever you finish a long rest, choose one of the following damage types:

**acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison.**

You gain resistance to the chosen damage type until the end of your next long rest.

While this resistance is active, the corresponding chromatic colour becomes dominant
across the scale.

## Sixth Light

When a creature succeeds on a saving throw as a result of **We Stand as Many**, the
scale flashes with the impossible sixth colour.

That creature gains temporary hit points equal to your **proficiency bonus + your
Charisma modifier** (minimum of 1).

These temporary hit points last until the creature finishes a short or long rest.

## Many Stand as One

When you expend the scale's **final charge**, choose one creature within 30 feet of you
that you can see.

That creature may immediately move up to **half its speed** without provoking
opportunity attacks.

This movement represents the scale's deeper purpose: not merely surviving alone, but
creating the space for another to survive with you.

## Draconic Concord

While attuned to the scale, you can speak, read and understand **Draconic**.

If you already know Draconic, you instead gain proficiency in one of the following
skills while attuned:

**History, Insight, or Persuasion.**

You choose the skill when you attune to the scale.

## Sentiment of the Scale

The Fivefold Scale is not sentient in the normal sense, but it carries an impression of
the ancient trial that birthed it.

It does not react to ordinary arguments.

It reacts to **division**.

When allies turn on one another in earnest, the five colours begin to pull apart,
leaving black seams between them.

When those same allies choose to stand together again, the colours slowly bleed back
into one another.

The scale does not demand harmony.

It demands **solidarity despite disagreement**.

## Hidden Inscription

The first time each member of the attuned wielder's adventuring party has been saved by
**We Stand as Many**, a second inscription appears beneath the first:

> **TIAMAT CREATED WITH FIVE.**
> **ELDERON BECAME MORE.**

Once this inscription appears, the item is considered **awakened** for the purposes of
future story progression.

## Awakened Legacy

The Fivefold Scale is currently in its **Dormant** state despite its very rare rarity.

At the DM's discretion, it can evolve through major acts of unity, sacrifice, or
world-shaping cooperation.

A later **Legendary** form could increase its charges, extend its range, allow rerolls
on attack rolls or ability checks made to save another creature, or briefly grant
multiple chromatic resistances at once.

## Foundry Summary

**Charges:** 3  
**Recharge:** Dawn  
**Trigger:** A creature within 30 ft fails a saving throw  
**Action Economy:** Reaction  
**Effect:** Reroll the save; new result must be used  
**Passive:** Choose acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison resistance after each long rest  
**On Successful Reroll:** Gain temporary HP equal to PB + CHA modifier  
**On Final Charge:** One creature within 30 ft moves up to half speed without provoking opportunity attacks
