Yes. I would make the **Pyramid Relics a matched collection of sentient Mimic Weapons**, with one eventually choosing each adventurer. At level 6, I would introduce them in a **Rare/Dormant state** rather than immediately giving everyone Very Rare or Legendary equipment. Their personalities and future evolutions can then grow through play.

The character tailoring below is based on the campaign record: Fick's fascination with grafting/symbiosis and self-sacrifice, Dolly's unusual relationship with Mimics and humanoids, Nuru's Goodberry obsession and chaotic competence, Bill/Slimon's ooze identity and willingness to sacrifice his own name, and Bitter's much harsher personality following Sour's death.   

# The Mimic Relics of the Pyramids

All six weapons share the following property.

### Living Relic

A Mimic Relic is a sentient magic item. While attuned to it, you cannot be disarmed of it unless you are incapacitated or the weapon willingly releases you.

Each relic can communicate telepathically with its attuned wielder within 120 feet. Most can also speak aloud by growing temporary mouths.

If a Mimic Relic is separated from its wielder for more than 24 hours, it can crawl at a speed of 10 feet and will generally attempt to find them.

Each weapon has **False Appearance**. While motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary weapon of its type unless it chooses otherwise.

These are not manufactured weapons.

They are creatures that have **chosen to become weapons**.

# 1. Bitter

# Tongue of the Last Wyrm

*Weapon (greatsword), rare (requires attunement)*

> The sword is unpleasant to look at even before it moves.
>
> A single golden eye sits where blade meets hilt. The cutting edge is lined with tiny backward-facing teeth like those of some impossible deep-sea predator. A wet crimson tongue periodically emerges between them and slowly drags itself along the blade.
>
> When Bitter touches it, the eye narrows.
>
> The tongue tastes the air.
>
> And a voice enters her mind.
>
> **"Oh. You're angry."**
>
> A pause.
>
> **"Good."**

This is the weapon already recovered from the Temple of Memories.

### Weapon

You gain a **+1 bonus to attack and damage rolls** made with this magic greatsword.

It deals magical slashing damage.

### Taste of Blood

Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature that is below half its hit point maximum, the sword deals an additional **1d6 acid damage**.

### Tongue Lash

As a bonus action, you can cause the sword's tongue to lash toward one Large or smaller creature within 15 feet.

The target must succeed on a **DC 14 Strength saving throw** or be pulled up to 10 feet toward you.

You can use this property a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus and regain all uses at dawn.

### Feed Me

When you reduce a hostile creature to 0 hit points with the sword, you gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus.

If the creature was a dragon, you instead gain **twice your proficiency bonus**.

### Sentience

**Intelligence 10, Wisdom 14, Charisma 16**

The sword can see and hear out to 120 feet.

It speaks Common, Draconic and Deep Speech.

### Personality

It is aggressive, sarcastic and completely uninterested in euphemisms.

It likes Bitter because Bitter does not require it to pretend that killing people is respectable.

Its greatest disagreement with her is that it considers nearly everything **food**.

### Hunger

Dragon flesh.

Powerful creatures.

Enemies who thought they were untouchable.

### Quirk

It quietly assesses creatures for edible qualities.

> "Too stringy."

> "That one smells expensive."

> "Don't burn this one. It ruins the texture."

### Killing Blow Phrases

**"NEXT."**

**"Bit chewy."**

**"Tastes better frightened."**

**"You weren't going to eat that, were you?"**

Against a dragon:

**"Tastes like history."**

# 2. Nuru

# The Berry Bad Idea

*Weapon (longbow), rare (requires attunement)*

> At first glance it looks like a dark wooden bow.
>
> Then the wood exhales.
>
> The bowstring is tendon.
>
> The arrow rest is a tiny tongue.
>
> Near the grip is a little mouth containing far too many teeth for something that size.
>
> When Nuru touches it, the weapon whispers:
>
> **"At last."**
>
> **"A tactical mastermind."**

### Weapon

You gain a **+1 bonus to attack and damage rolls** made with this magic longbow.

The weapon creates its own arrows. Whenever you draw the string, a wet organic arrow grows along it and dissolves shortly after hitting or missing.

### Tactical Genius

Once per turn when you hit a creature with the bow, choose one:

**Sticky Shot.** The creature's speed is reduced by 10 feet until the beginning of your next turn.

**Distraction Shot.** The next attack roll made against the creature before the beginning of your next turn has advantage.

**Absolutely Deliberate Shot.** Push the creature 5 feet.

### Goodberry Ammunition

As a bonus action, you may feed the bow a Goodberry.

The next arrow fired within 1 minute can target a willing creature instead of an enemy.

On a hit, it deals no damage and restores **1d6 + your proficiency bonus hit points**.

The target is still technically shot with a berry.

### I Meant To Do That

When you roll a natural 1 on an attack with this weapon, you can reroll the attack.

You must use the new roll.

Once this property is used, it cannot be used again until the next dawn.

### Sentience

**Intelligence 13, Wisdom 8, Charisma 15**

It speaks Common and Sylvan and communicates telepathically.

### Personality

The bow is absolutely convinced Nuru is secretly one of the greatest tactical minds in Elderon's history.

Nothing can persuade it otherwise.

Accidental friendly fire?

**Testing allied defences.**

Picking his nose during negotiations?

**Psychological warfare.**

Running the wrong direction?

**Flanking manoeuvre.**

Nuru's mix of Goodberries, friendly-fire disasters and occasional unexpectedly effective support is a recurring part of the campaign. 

### Hunger

Goodberries.

It develops increasingly pretentious opinions about flavours.

> "Blackberry again?"

> "We are adventurers, Nuru, not livestock."

### Killing Blow Phrases

**"Exactly as planned."**

**"They never saw your genius coming."**

**"Perfect trajectory. Ignore the first arrow."**

**"Tactical berry superiority confirmed."**

Natural 1 followed by a successful reroll:

**"Phase Two."**

# 3. Fick

# The Kindly Anatomist

*Weapon (sickle), rare (requires attunement by a creature capable of spellcasting)*

> The object looks less forged than grown.
>
> A curved blade of ivory emerges from a bundle of black tendons. Tiny sensory fronds twitch along the handle.
>
> When Fick takes hold of it, several tendrils pierce painlessly into the wrist.
>
> The weapon gives a small shudder.
>
> Then:
>
> **"Oh."**
>
> **"You've already started modifying yourself."**
>
> Another pause.
>
> **"I like you."**

### Weapon

You gain a **+1 bonus to attack and damage rolls** made with this sickle.

You may use your **Wisdom modifier** instead of Strength for its attack and damage rolls.

It can be used as a druidic spellcasting focus.

### Harvest Anatomy

When a creature that you damaged with this weapon dies within 30 feet of you, you may use your reaction to allow the Anatomist to sample it.

Choose one adaptation appropriate to the creature:

**Carapace.** +1 AC until your next long rest.

**Predator's Legs.** Your walking speed increases by 10 feet.

**Aquatic Tissue.** You gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed and can breathe underwater.

**Clinging Tissue.** You gain a climbing speed equal to your walking speed.

**Night Organ.** You gain darkvision to 60 feet, or increase existing darkvision by 30 feet.

You can maintain only **one harvested adaptation at a time**.

The DM may create equivalent adaptations for particularly unusual anatomy.

### Experimental Graft

Once per long rest, when you finish a short rest you may allow the weapon to graft one harvested biological component onto you.

Choose one damage type dealt by the sampled creature.

You gain resistance to that damage type for **1 hour**.

### Surgical Precision

Once per turn, when you damage a creature with the sickle, you can deal an additional **1d4 necrotic or acid damage**.

### Sentience

**Intelligence 17, Wisdom 12, Charisma 8**

It speaks Common, Druidic and Deep Speech.

### Personality

Extremely intelligent.

Completely lacking a normal sense of disgust.

It doesn't say:

> "That's horrifying."

It says:

> "Interesting vascular arrangement."

Fick's history makes this relic particularly dangerous because it doesn't need to convince Fick that grafting bizarre anatomy is a good idea. Fick is already fascinated by transformation and incorporates biological traits harvested from creatures. 

### Hunger

New biology.

It wants a sample of **everything**.

### Quirk

It constantly proposes unnecessary improvements.

> "Retractable eyelids."

> "I already have eyelids."

> "But these retract sideways."

### Killing Blow Phrases

**"Specimen secured."**

**"Waste nothing."**

**"Oh, that's useful."**

**"You weren't using that anymore."**

For an aberration:

**"Fick. FICK. You need to see this."**

# 4. Bill Cruzby

# Everyone's Invited

*Weapon (quarterstaff), rare (requires attunement)*

> It initially resembles a polished black staff.
>
> Then Bill sets it down.
>
> It slumps.
>
> Slowly.
>
> Like a candle in the sun.
>
> A pseudopod reaches toward his leg.
>
> **"Hello."**
>
> **"Are you also mostly liquid?"**
>
> The staff quivers excitedly.
>
> **"We're going to be excellent friends."**

### Weapon

You gain a **+1 bonus to attack and damage rolls** made with this quarterstaff.

It can serve as a spellcasting focus.

### Amorphous Weapon

At the beginning of your turn, choose one form.

**Staff.** Normal quarterstaff.

**Whip.** The weapon gains reach and deals 1d6 slashing damage.

**Hammer.** The weapon deals 1d8 bludgeoning damage one-handed.

The weapon remains magical regardless of form.

### Join Us

Once per turn when you deal damage to a creature with either this weapon or a spell attack while holding it, you can cover the target in adhesive slime.

Its speed is reduced by **10 feet** until the beginning of your next turn.

### Family Support

When a creature you can see within 10 feet is hit by an attack, you may use your reaction to stretch part of the weapon around it.

Reduce the damage by **1d8 + your proficiency bonus**.

You can use this ability a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Puddle Mode

As an action, the weapon liquefies into a sticky sheet covering one unoccupied 10-foot-square surface within reach.

Until you use a bonus action to recall it, friendly creatures can move across that surface normally, while enemies treat it as difficult terrain.

It can form a temporary rope, ladder, bridge or adhesive patch at the DM's discretion.

### Sentience

**Intelligence 9, Wisdom 13, Charisma 18**

It speaks Common, Primordial and Deep Speech.

### Personality

Optimistic to a genuinely alarming degree.

It believes assimilation solves most disagreements.

Bill's own relationship with Ghaunadaur and his increasingly ooze-centred worldview make this relic less a corrupting influence and more an enthusiastic collaborator. The campaign notes describe Bill/Slimon's growing connection with the ooze god and his surrender of his own name during the Temple trial. 

### Hunger

Other slimes.

But it insists it isn't eating them.

It is **making friends**.

### Quirk

It keeps giving Bill new names.

> "Bill."

> "Slimothy."

> "William Goozby."

> "His Moistness."

### Killing Blow Phrases

**"Welcome to the family!"**

**"Integration successful."**

**"You seem happier as goo."**

**"One of us!"**

Against an ooze:

**"...Cousin?"**

# 5. Dolly

# The Seam Ripper

*Weapon (rapier), rare (requires attunement)*

> It resembles an enormous sewing needle scaled into a duelling weapon.
>
> The polished black point has no conventional edge.
>
> A length of crimson thread trails from the eye.
>
> Except the thread occasionally licks things.
>
> When Dolly touches it, the needle immediately examines one of her damaged seams.
>
> **"Oh dear."**
>
> **"Who stitched you?"**
>
> An offended pause.
>
> **"We'll fix that."**

### Weapon

You gain a **+1 bonus to attack and damage rolls** made with this magic rapier.

### Loose Thread

When you deal Sneak Attack damage to a creature, you may cause a living thread to shoot from the weapon.

The target must make a **DC 14 Strength saving throw**.

On a failure, choose one:

* its speed becomes 0 until the beginning of your next turn;
* it is pulled 10 feet toward you;
* if it is adjacent to a wall, floor, creature or large object, it becomes restrained until the beginning of your next turn.

You can use this property a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Stitch It Closed

As a bonus action, touch yourself or another creature within reach.

Living thread sews wounds together and restores **2d6 + your proficiency bonus hit points**.

Once used, this property cannot be used again until the next dawn.

It looks extremely unpleasant.

### Patchwork Escape

When a creature hits you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to unravel into loose cloth, stuffing and Mimic tissue.

You may move up to **10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks**.

Once this property is used, it cannot be used again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Sentience

**Intelligence 12, Wisdom 16, Charisma 14**

It speaks Common and understands Deep Speech.

### Personality

Kind.

Helpful.

Polite.

Utterly horrifying.

Dolly learned only relatively recently that she was a Mimic and has shown little automatic loyalty toward Mimic society simply because they share a creature type. 

The Seam Ripper thinks this is perfectly sensible.

### Hunger

Thread.

Buttons.

Interesting fabric.

Clothing belonging to memorable people.

### Quirk

It repairs Dolly while she sleeps.

Sometimes it adds improvements she did not request.

Tiny bows.

Embroidery.

An unnecessary pocket.

### Killing Blow Phrases

**"Snip snip."**

**"Oh dear. You've come undone."**

**"That seam wasn't structural, was it?"**

**"Lovely fabric."**

And occasionally:

**"I'll put you back together later."**

# 6. The Newcomer

# The Unchosen

*Weapon (any simple or martial weapon), rare (requires attunement)*

I would deliberately make the sixth relic different because the newest character has nowhere near the amount of campaign history the others have yet.

Its **mechanics are complete**, but its personality isn't.

That becomes the gimmick.

> Among the grotesque living treasures of the pyramid lies one completely ordinary weapon.
>
> No eye.
>
> No teeth.
>
> No tongue.
>
> No breathing.
>
> Nothing.
>
> When lifted, however, two words appear along its surface:
>
> **NOT YET.**

### Weapon

When first attuned, choose any simple or martial weapon with which you are proficient.

The Unchosen permanently assumes that form.

You gain a **+1 bonus to attack and damage rolls** with it.

### Learning You

Whenever you finish a long rest after having:

* reduced a hostile creature to 0 hit points;
* saved an ally from dropping to 0 hit points;
* failed an important ability check;
* succeeded on an important ability check;
* acted directly according to one of your ideals, bonds or flaws;

the DM may mark one **Observation**.

At **3 Observations**, the weapon awakens.

At that point its voice, Hunger, quirks and personalized ability are created from how that character has actually behaved in play.

### Adaptive Relic

Until it awakens, once per long rest when you fail an attack roll, ability check or saving throw, the weapon may quietly say:

> **"Interesting."**

You can reroll the d20.

You must use the new result.

### Sentience

Until awakened:

**Intelligence 18, Wisdom 18, Charisma 1**

It understands all languages.

It simply refuses to speak.

### Killing Blow Phrase

Before awakening, it says nothing.

Except perhaps, after one particularly revealing kill:

> **"...Noted."**

That makes the sixth item increasingly creepy while allowing you to personalize it from actual sessions instead of guessing who the new PC is going to become.

# How I Would Place Them in the Pyramids

I wouldn't make each one simply sit on a pedestal.

The Mimic Relics should **choose the characters through encounters**.

**Northern Pyramid — Tongue of the Last Wyrm.** Bitter's sword is the first recovered relic and establishes the concept.

**Second Pyramid — The Berry Bad Idea.** Hidden inside a dead tree whose fruit are tiny sleeping Mimics. The bow only wakes after Nuru feeds it a Goodberry.

**Third Pyramid — The Kindly Anatomist.** Found inside the corpse of an ancient aberration. Fick thinks he's harvesting an organ until the organ asks him to be more careful.

**Fourth Pyramid — Everyone's Invited.** Appears to be a black pool blocking progress. Bill attempts to communicate with it. The pool follows him afterward.

**Fifth Pyramid — The Seam Ripper.** Found in an ancient room filled with dolls representing extinct Elderonian peoples. One needle keeps moving whenever Dolly looks away.

**Final Pyramid — The Unchosen.** Completely mundane-looking among a room full of impossible treasure. Every other Mimic object tries to prevent the newcomer from touching it.

# The Important Campaign Mechanic: Awakening

I would put one final paragraph on **every item's D&D Beyond/Foundry description**:

### Awakening

This weapon is in its Dormant state. It is not merely an object but a developing creature. Its statistics may change when significant events strengthen the bond between weapon and wielder. Feeding the weapon, following or rejecting its advice, allowing it to form relationships with the party, discovering its memories, and confronting the purpose for which it was created may awaken additional properties.

That gives you permission in-world to upgrade them from:

**Rare at levels 6–8 → Very Rare around 9–12 → Legendary in the endgame.**

And when the second one is recovered, Bitter's Greatsword should immediately stop talking in the middle of a sentence, turn its golden eye toward the newly discovered relic and say:

> **"...You."**

The other weapon answers:

> **"Oh."**
>
> **"You survived."**

That is when the players realise these aren't six unrelated Mimics.

**They knew each other before the world ended.**
