Yes. The cleanest way to make the pyramids work is to treat them not simply as nine dungeons containing nine weapons, but as **one planetary machine divided into nine sacred fortresses**.

Your established lore already gives us the foundation: the Pindarians were divided into the Children of the Sun, Moon and Void; they built multidimensional cities through planar magic; the demonic invasion killed millions; and the surviving royal family and soldiers used dark magic to become deathless guardians of the pyramid network.  The later campaign establishes that this network once prevented **fiends and celestials alike from physically entering Elderon**, and that disturbing one of its weapons helped allow the catastrophe that ultimately became the First Hunger. 

The nine weapons you established—Velvet Ruin, Feastchain, Gravewake, The Two That Are One, The Labyrinth's Answer, The Bride of Rot, Mercy of Dissolution, The Honest Lie, and Winter's Grudge—fit this beautifully. 

What follows is how I would now make the **Nine Pyramids definitive Elderon canon**.

# THE NINE PYRAMIDS OF PINDAR

### The Ninefold Prohibition

The Pindarians called the completed barrier:

**The Asha'Vael Tharos**
*“The Boundary That Even Gods Must Ask to Cross.”*

Modern scholars simply call it **the Ninefold Prohibition**.

The pyramids do not physically push the Outer Planes away.

They do something much stranger.

They tell reality:

> **This world is complete. Nothing from outside belongs here.**

Seven pyramids were constructed upon Elderon's most important metaphysical convergence points.

Two were placed outside Elderon in the Astral Sea.

Together they formed a closed metaphysical shell.

The nine demonic weapons were used as **resonance keys**.

The Pindarians discovered that a fragment carrying the metaphysical signature of an Abyssal Prince could be used like a tuning fork. By defining the most extreme examples of extraplanar existence, the pyramids could identify anything whose essential soul originated outside Elderon's Material Plane.

Demon.

Devil.

Celestial.

Archon.

Yugoloth.

Even a god attempting direct manifestation.

The system did not care whether the being was good or evil.

It only asked:

> **Were you born of Elderon?**

If the answer was no, the Ninefold Prohibition resisted manifestation.

Portals could still exist. Spirits could communicate. Gods could answer prayer. Astral travel remained possible.

But armies could not simply march into Elderon.

That distinction is important.

The Pindarians had not isolated themselves from the multiverse.

They had made Elderon **sovereign territory on a metaphysical level.**

# THE DEMONIC INVASION

The invasion was not an accident.

It was a conspiracy.

The Churches of **Bane** helped guide the Gith toward the demonic powers that ultimately attacked Elderon. Graz'zt and the Witch Queen Iggwilv became central figures in the resulting invasion. Graz'zt had a deeper plan: he wanted Bahamut, Tiamat and the Pindarians desperate enough to create a permanent planar prison, because he intended Orcus and Demogorgon to become trapped by it. 

Graz'zt therefore did something characteristic of Graz'zt.

He started a war he had no intention of winning.

He merely needed everyone else to lose.

The hordes came through Pindarian portals.

Yeenoghu's packs devastated settlements.

Zuggtmoy seeded forests and agricultural lands.

Juiblex dissolved fortifications.

Baphomet's servants hunted through cities whose streets had been transformed into labyrinths.

Fraz-Urb'luu filled governments with false information.

Orcus raised those killed in earlier battles.

Demogorgon's armies turned conquered populations against one another.

And behind them came Graz'zt.

Millions died.

Entire Pindarian multidimensional cities collapsed.

Eventually the High Priestesses of the Pindarian Empire gathered with the **Twelve Elder Dragons**, led by Bahamut and Tiamat.

They devised the Ninefold Prohibition.

But they needed something that could identify the Abyss.

They therefore captured, stole or created sympathetic fragments of nine Demon Princes.

Those became the **Nine Demon Weapons**.

The irony was perfect:

**the power of the Abyss became the thing that locked the Abyss outside.**

---

# THE ETERNAL WATCH

The Pindarians knew eventually somebody would try to steal the weapons.

So the surviving royal families, generals, priests and scholars made their final sacrifice.

They became the **Saharim**.

### The Deathless Watch.

Their immortality resembles lichdom, but it is not identical.

A Saharim's soul is not placed inside a conventional phylactery.

Instead, each guardian's soul is bound into the pyramid itself.

Kill the guardian and the pyramid reconstructs them.

Destroy their body and another forms.

Disintegrate them and the dust begins crawling toward the nearest wall.

Their immortality ends only when:

1. their pyramid's weapon is lawfully released;
2. the guardian voluntarily relinquishes the Watch;
3. or the pyramid itself is permanently destroyed.

This explains why the guardians are still present thousands of years later.

It also means they aren't simply bosses.

Some desperately **want to be allowed to die**.

---

# THE PURPOSE OF THE TRIALS

The pyramids weren't designed to keep everyone out.

Eventually there might come a time when the weapons were needed.

Therefore every pyramid asks essentially the same question:

> **Can you possess this weapon without becoming what it represents?**

Each pyramid represents a Demon Prince's philosophy.

But its trials represent the **opposite virtue**.

That is the genius of their design.

The weapon of Desire is protected by Sacrifice.

The weapon of Hunger is protected by Mercy.

The weapon of Death is protected by Acceptance.

And so forth.

# Pindarian Physiology

The Pindarians were not human, and the single most common mistake made by scholars working from their
architecture is to assume they were.

## Size

**Every Pindarian is Large.** Eight to ten feet standing, long-boned, narrow through the shoulder relative
to their height, and unhurried in a way that reads as arrogance and is mostly just leverage. This is uniform
across all four peoples — Solaari, Lunari, Veylari and Ilyri — and it does not vary by tradition, rank or
role. A Pindarian infant is the size of a human child of ten.

In game terms: **Large, 10-foot space, 10-foot reach on melee attacks, and d10 hit dice.** The Saharim
guardians retain their living dimensions; four thousand years of the Watch has hollowed several of them out
without making any of them shorter.

## What This Looks Like at the Table

The nine sites were built by and for people half again the size of the party, and the best way to establish
that is through furniture rather than exposition.

- **Doorways** run eleven to twelve feet. Corridors are wide and high and feel wasteful.
- **Stairs** have deep treads and high risers. A Medium creature climbing a Pindarian stair is doing a step
  and a half every time.
- **Chairs, benches, beds and tables** do not fit anyone else. The reading desks in the Archive of Unsent
  Letters seat somebody nine feet tall; the bed in the Mirrored Hospice is eight feet long; the throne in
  Astra's crown chamber is scaled for its occupant and a human sitting in it does not reach the arms.
- **Handholds, levers, latches and pull-rings** are set at chest height for a Pindarian, which is head
  height for most parties, and several of the site guides' puzzles are easier for a tall character and
  should be allowed to be.
- **Armour and weapons** recovered from a Pindarian site are sized for a Large wielder. A Medium creature
  using a Pindarian weapon two-handed does so with disadvantage unless its Strength is 18 or higher, at the
  GM's discretion, and Pindarian plate simply does not go on.

## What They Were Not

They were not giants, and no Pindarian source uses the word. They had no giant ancestry, no giant kinship,
and the Solaari fought frost giants for two centuries with no sense of fighting a relative.

They were not elves either, whatever the surviving portraits suggest to a modern eye. The resemblance is a
coincidence of proportion and longevity, and it has caused four thousand years of bad scholarship.

## Everyone Else

Humans, elves, dwarves, halflings, minotaurs, gnolls and everyone else the party meets in and around the
nine sites are Medium as usual. **The contrast is the point**, and a party that has spent nine pyramids
ducking through Pindarian doorways should feel it the first time they stand in an ordinary room again.
